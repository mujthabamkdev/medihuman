MediHuman helps patients and caregivers organise medication information for clearer care conversations.

Core flow:
- Review medication information.
- Capture a prescription using camera/photo or manual details.
- Review captured text against the original.
- Compare prescribed and dispensed medicines.
- Organise profile, medicines, prescriptions, comparisons, and questions.
- Choose clinician access using a private patient ID and explicit approval.

Safety boundaries:
- MediHuman does not diagnose, prescribe, validate handwriting, or replace clinician or pharmacist advice.
- Do not start, stop, or change a dose based only on this app.
- Images are processed in the browser and are not uploaded here.

Implementation proof:
- Normalized Supabase tables.
- Service-role-only persistence with row-level security.
- Distributed rate limiting.
