## Intent

Create a 90-second buildathon submission that explains the medication-information problem, demonstrates MediHuman's patient workflow, and proves the implementation is privacy-conscious and production-minded. Show the real app as the primary visual source. Tone: clear, credible, calm, and practical.

## Assets

- http://localhost:4173 — running MediHuman app; capture the real interface and primary user flow.
- src/icon.svg — MediHuman brand mark.

## Customizations

- Professional neutral voice-over.
- Matching calm instrumental background music: soft piano and light strings,
  mixed beneath narration with a smooth opening and ending.
- On-screen captions for judge-friendly silent playback.
- Show stored-browser mode, profile/medicine organisation, prescription capture/review, reconciliation, privacy controls, and clinician access boundary.
- Include concise architecture proof: normalized Supabase tables, RLS/service-role boundary, and distributed rate limiting.
- Use app screens and focused callouts rather than generic stock footage.

## Notes

- Do not imply diagnosis, prescribing, clinical validation, or treatment recommendations.
- Keep patient data fictional or empty during capture.
- Prioritize one coherent story over exhaustive feature coverage.
